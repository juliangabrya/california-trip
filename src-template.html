The Waymo rule, straight up

Waymo's own policy: riders must be 18+ to ride alone. Teen accounts exist only for ages 14–17 and only in Phoenix so far. A 13-year-old riding solo on a parent's account breaks the terms of service and can get that account suspended. This plan is built so every solo day works on Caltrain + walking — Waymo is for the days an adult is riding along. Bonus rule that works in your favor: regular Muni is free for everyone 18 and under, visitors included — just get on (cable cars excepted: $9 flat). Solo-day kit: charged phone + power bank, live location shared, proof of age for the youth fares. Trains run every 30 minutes, so a missed one is never a crisis.

Thu Aug 20 · Northstar, the full send

  • 08:30

    Free hot breakfast at the lodge (6:30–9:30 — eggs, bacon, the pancake machine). Stopped short last night? Finish the drive now; you'll still beat the lifts.

  • 09:15

    To Northstar: I-80 east → exit 188B → CA-267 to Northstar Drive, ~20 min. Summer parking is free, no reservation. Pick up pre-booked downhill bikes + armor in the village.

  • 10:00

    Lifts spin 10:00–17:00 (Big Springs Gondola → Vista Express → Tahoe Zephyr) — a full day, first chair to last.

  • flow

    You ride flow — this is your mountain. Northstar's calling card is Livewire: the famous machine-built flow/jump line, freshly rebuilt for 2026 — berms, tables, rollers top to bottom. Warm up on the green/blue machine-groomed flow lines first lap, then session Livewire the rest of the day; grab the trail map at the rental shop and ask the crew for the current flow progression (they'll point you to which blues feed into it).First lift-served day ever? The Bike 101 package (ticket + gear + lesson) gets you dialed by noon.

  • 17:00

    Back to the lodge, pizza in downtown Truckee, collapse.

Why Thursday is the day: the California Enduro Series race takes over parts of the mountain from Friday's course practice through the weekend. Thursday is the cleanest, quietest park day of the week — send it, first chair to last.

Sat Aug 22 · The Tiburon loop

  • 08:15

    Drive in from Millbrae (~25 min) and hand back the Tahoe rental car at the Wharf: Hertz / Avis at 500 Beach St or Enterprise at 350 Beach St, open ~08:00, three blocks from the bike shop.Check the downtown-drop / one-way fee when booking the car at the airport.

  • 09:00

    Rent at Blazing Saddles, 2715 Hyde St (opens 08:00). Throttle plan: the "E-Blazer" e-bike — pedal-assist plus power-on-demand throttle, $80/day ($64 booked online). Class 2, so it's legal at 13 (California has no minimum age below Class 3), riders must be 5'0"+, parent signs for under-18s. Helmet and lock included — helmets are the law under 18.Skip their Rad cargo e-bikes (65–77 lb — over the ferry limit). Backup shop with throttle e-bikes: Marina Bike Shop, 3749A Buchanan St, $89/day incl. a kids' 24" e-bike.

  • 10:00

    Roll: Wharf → Crissy Field → up to the bridge (4 mi) → across the Golden Gate (1.7 mi) → swoop down into Sausalito (2.8 mi).Saturday perk: bikes get the west sidewalk to themselves all day — no dodging pedestrians. 15 mph limit, 5 mph at the towers.

  • 12:30

    Lunch in Sausalito, then the flat, pretty Mill Valley–Sausalito path onward to Tiburon (~7 mi). ~18 mi total, ~3 h of actual riding.

  • 14:15

    Be at the Tiburon ferry dock 30 min early — bike space is first-come. Golden Gate Ferry to SF: 14:45 (scenic, via Angel Island, lands 15:50) or the last boat at 17:15. Bikes ride free.

  • 16:00

    Land at the Ferry Building, easy 1.5 mi flat cruise up the Embarcadero to return the bikes. Wharf dinner + a wander through Musée Mécanique (free, open late Saturdays — bring quarters).

Ferry fares: adults tap any contactless card ($8.50). The kid uses Friday's $7 youth ticket / Youth Clipper — Tiburon has no machine (Sausalito's terminal has one if you forgot, or just tap a contactless card and eat the $1.50 difference). Miss the 17:15? Ride 7 flat miles back to Sausalito for the 18:55.
E-bike fine print: Golden Gate Ferry takes e-bikes under 50 lb only (power off on board; Tiburon boats carry max 30 bikes). If the E-Blazer turns out heavier, two outs: do Tiburon on standard pedal-assist e-bikes, or keep the throttles and end the ride in Sausalito instead — Blue & Gold Fleet's Pier 41 ferry publishes no e-bike weight limit, bikes ride free, Blazing Saddles sells discounted tickets, and it lands 5 minutes from the shop (confirm heavy e-bikes on 415-705-8200). And to be clear on the Sur-Ron idea: that's legally an off-highway motorcycle — banned from the bridge and the bay path. Your downhill fix already happened at Northstar.

Check uss-hornet.org/events before going — the ship occasionally hosts special-event days. Total aviation-day damage for three people: ~$75 in tickets vs $350 for one demo flight.
